# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Participants must communicate professionally, discuss technical issues on their merits, respect differing experience levels, and provide evidence for claims that affect project behavior or safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

The project does not tolerate harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, credential exposure, or knowingly fabricated technical evidence.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and other spaces represented as part of EvidenceLoop.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private security-reporting channel or another private contact method listed by the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters or affected participants.
